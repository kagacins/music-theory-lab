# Quick Deploy to Netlify (Drag & Drop Method)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Prepare Your Files
- Make sure all your files are in the project folder
- No changes needed - your app is ready to deploy!

### 2. Deploy to Netlify
1. **Open your web browser**
2. **Go to**: https://app.netlify.com/drop
   - (If you don't have an account, sign up - it's free!)
3. **Drag your entire project folder** onto the page
   - Drag the folder: `Music Project Refactored`
   - Wait for upload to complete (10-30 seconds)
4. **Get your live URL!**
   - Netlify will show you a URL like: `https://amazing-music-12345.netlify.app`
   - Your site is now live! 🎉

### 3. (Optional) Customize Your URL
1. Click on your site in Netlify dashboard
2. Go to **Site settings** → **Change site name**
3. Choose a custom name (e.g., `music-theory-lab`)
4. Your new URL: `https://music-theory-lab.netlify.app`

### 4. (Optional) Add a Custom Domain
1. Go to **Domain settings** → **Add custom domain**
2. Enter your domain (e.g., `musictheorylab.com`)
3. Follow DNS setup instructions
4. Netlify provides free SSL certificate automatically

---

## That's It!

Your Music Theory Lab is now live on the internet and accessible to anyone with the URL!
